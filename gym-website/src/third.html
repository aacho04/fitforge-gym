How to Join PowerFit

From sign up to your first class, here's exactly what happens

1

Create your account

Register with your name, email, phone and a password. This takes less than a minute and gives you a member profile.

2

Choose your plan and pay

Pick Basic, Pro or Elite. Pay online with UPI, card or net banking, or choose Pay at gym and settle at reception. Your plan and payment status show up on your profile.

3

Pick your diet plan

Choose a Weight Loss or Weight Gain nutrition plan so your eating matches your training goal.

4

Book your first class

Browse HIIT, Yoga, Strength or Zumba and reserve a slot. You'll see it under "My bookings" on your profile.

5

Show up and mark attendance

Train, then mark your attendance from your profile. Your monthly attendance calendar tracks every visit.
